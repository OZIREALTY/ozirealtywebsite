What services can you help me with?

You can come to us for help with buying, selling, renting, property management, property appraisals, and investment guidance. Whether you're planning a move, growing your portfolio, or just exploring your options, you’ll get practical advice tailored to your goals.

What’s the easiest way to reach Ozi Realty?

You can contact us by phone or email, and we’ll point you to the right person for your needs: 📞 1800 400 333 ✉️ General Inquiries: support@ozirealty.com.au ✉️ Buyers: buy@ozirealty.com.au ✉️ Sellers: sell@ozirealty.com.au ✉️ Rentals: rent@ozirealty.com.au Or use our Contact Us page to send a message or request a call.

Thinking about selling? How does Ozi Realty support you?

You get a tailored sales strategy from the start. We can prepare a data-backed appraisal, market your property to the right buyers, highlight its best features, and guide you through negotiation and settlement. The goal is to make the process clearer, smoother, and more effective for you.

I’m looking for a rental property. Where can I view listings?

You can explore our available rental properties anytime at ozirealty.com.au Each listing includes detailed information, images, and options to enquire or apply online.

Do you offer help with overseas property investment?

Yes — if you're looking at overseas property, we can help you explore opportunities and understand the key risks, costs, and next steps. You’ll get market insights, practical guidance, and support through the buying process, including selected markets like Dubai.

Is overseas property investment right for you?

It can be, but it depends on your goals, budget, and risk comfort. Overseas property can offer strong opportunities, but you should always weigh the costs, market conditions, and risks first. We’ll help you assess the numbers and make a clear, informed decision.
