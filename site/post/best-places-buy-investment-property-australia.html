Best Places to Buy Investment Property in Australia

12 Aug 2026 · 4 min read · Ozi Realty

Updated: Aug 17

If you're weighing up where to put your money into property, you've probably noticed Australia gives you no shortage of choices. From the bright lights of Sydney to the steady growth of the smaller capitals, every market has its own story. The trick is finding the place where your budget, your goals and the local fundamentals all line up.

I've worked with plenty of investors over the years, and the ones who do well rarely chase the loudest headline. They look for the quieter markets with strong rental demand, sensible entry prices and room to grow. So let's walk through where those opportunities are sitting right now, and how to think about them.

What actually makes a location worth investing in

Before we name suburbs and cities, it helps to know what you're really looking for. A good investment location usually shares a few traits: steady population growth, a healthy jobs market, infrastructure that's being built or upgraded, and a rental vacancy rate that's low enough to keep tenants competing for homes.

Affordability matters too. A cheaper entry price often means a stronger rental yield, which keeps your holding costs manageable while the property grows in value. Balancing yield and long-term capital growth is the heart of every smart decision, and it's something we dig into properly in our Adelaide property investment guide .

The capital cities worth a closer look

Adelaide, South Australia

I'll be honest about my home turf: Adelaide has quietly become one of the most compelling markets in the country. Entry prices remain far kinder than Sydney or Melbourne, rental demand is strong, and vacancy rates have stayed tight. Inner-north suburbs like Prospect, with their character homes and leafy streets, continue to attract both tenants and owner-occupiers, which supports long-term value.

Brisbane and South East Queensland

Brisbane has drawn a steady stream of interstate movers chasing warmer weather and relative affordability. Major infrastructure spending and lifestyle appeal have kept demand firm, and the wider South East Queensland region offers plenty of options for investors who want growth without a Sydney-sized price tag.

Perth, Western Australia

Perth spent years out of the spotlight, and that's exactly why some investors have turned back to it. Prices reset over the past decade, yields are attractive, and a resurgent resources sector has supported jobs and population growth. It's a market that rewards doing your homework.

Sydney and Melbourne

The two biggest cities still hold their place for a reason: deep, diverse economies and long track records of capital growth. The catch is the price of entry, which pushes rental yields lower. They can suit investors focused on long-term value in blue-chip areas, but they ask for deeper pockets up front.

Why Adelaide keeps punching above its weight

If you'd asked me a decade ago which city offered the best balance of affordability and growth, I might have hesitated. Today it's an easy answer. Adelaide combines a lower cost of entry with genuine lifestyle appeal, a growing population and consistent rental demand, which is a rare mix.

For investors buying from interstate or overseas, that stability is a real drawcard. It's why we built out our expert Adelaide investment property services to help buyers who can't be here in person make confident, well-researched decisions.

Looking beyond the big cities

Capital cities aren't the only game in town. Regional centres with strong local economies, universities or health precincts can deliver solid yields and lower entry costs. The key is to make sure the demand is real and durable, not just a short-lived spike, so you're not left holding a property that's hard to rent.

Australia also remains a genuinely attractive destination for buyers based abroad, thanks to its stable economy and transparent property system. If that's you, our guidance on opportunities for international investors is a good place to start.

How to choose the right property once you've picked a location

Picking the right city is only half the job. Within any market, the individual property matters just as much: its position, its condition, the type of tenant it will attract and the numbers behind it. This is where a clear plan pays off, and where our property strategy services can help you match a purchase to your goals rather than buying on gut feel.

And if you're buying from outside Australia, having a local team on the ground makes all the difference. Our overseas investment services cover everything from sourcing the right property to managing it once it's yours, so distance never becomes a disadvantage.

Ready to make your move?

The best places to buy investment property in Australia are the ones where the fundamentals stack up and the numbers make sense for you. Adelaide, with its affordability and steady demand, is a market I'd happily put forward, but the right answer always depends on your goals.
