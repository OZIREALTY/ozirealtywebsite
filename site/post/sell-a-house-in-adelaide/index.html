How to Sell a House in Adelaide: A Step-by-Step Guide

10 Aug 2026 · 4 min read · Ozi Realty

Thinking about selling your house in Adelaide? It's a big move, but it really doesn't have to be stressful. With the right preparation and the right team beside you, you can sell with confidence and land a result you're genuinely happy with.

Whether you're upsizing, downsizing or moving on from an investment, the way you approach the sale has a real impact on your final price and how quickly you reach settlement. Here's a friendly, step-by-step guide to selling a house in Adelaide, from your very first appraisal right through to handing over the keys.

Start with a realistic property appraisal

Before anything else, it helps to know what your home is genuinely worth in today's Adelaide market. Guessing, or going off what a neighbour sold for a couple of years ago, is one of the quickest ways to end up disappointed.

A professional appraisal looks at recent comparable sales in your suburb, current buyer demand and the features that make your property special. Booking a free property appraisal with our team is the easiest way to get an accurate, up-to-date figure to plan around. If you'd like to understand how those numbers are worked out, our guide to property valuation methods in Adelaide breaks it all down in plain language.

One friendly word of caution: be a little wary of the highest figure you hear. Some agents quote an inflated price simply to win your listing, then talk you down once the buyers don't appear. A realistic price almost always sells faster, and often for more.

Choose the right agent to sell your Adelaide home

The agent you choose shapes your whole selling experience, so it's worth comparing a few before you decide. Ask how well they know your suburb, how they plan to market your home, what their recent results look like, and exactly how their fees and commission work.

A local agent who sells regularly in your area understands what Adelaide buyers are really looking for and how to position your home against the competition. Good communication matters just as much as results, so choose someone who keeps you in the loop at every stage rather than leaving you wondering.

Prepare and present your home for sale

First impressions count, and most buyers form an opinion within seconds of stepping through the front door. A little effort here can add real dollars to your final price.

Declutter, give everything a deep clean and tackle those small repairs you've been putting off — a dripping tap or a scuffed wall can make a whole home feel neglected. Fresh paint, tidy gardens and plenty of natural light go a long way, and for many sellers a little professional styling or furniture hire more than pays for itself.

Set the right price and choose your sale method

Pricing is where many Adelaide sales are quietly won or lost. Price too high and your home lingers and grows stale; price it well and you create genuine competition among buyers.

You'll usually choose between auction and private treaty. Auctions create urgency and a clear deadline, which suits in-demand suburbs and standout homes. Private treaty, with a set asking price, gives buyers more time to consider and can suit quieter markets or more unique properties. A good agent will recommend the method that fits your home, your timeframe and current buyer activity, rather than a one-size-fits-all approach. If you'd like tailored advice, our property strategy services can help you map out the best plan for your sale.

Market your property where Adelaide buyers are looking

Great marketing puts your home in front of as many qualified buyers as possible. Professional photography, a well-written listing, a floor plan and prominent placement on the major property portals are all essential.

Layer in social media, email campaigns to active buyers and quality signage, and your pool widens even further. The more genuine interest you generate early, the stronger your position will be when the offers start coming in.

Contracts, cooling-off and settlement in South Australia

Once you accept an offer, the legal side begins. In South Australia, a sale needs a signed contract and a Form 1 (Vendor's Statement) that discloses key information about the property, and buyers usually have a two clear business day cooling-off period.

Your conveyancer and agent will guide you through the paperwork, the deposit and the settlement timeline so nothing slips through the cracks. Settlement usually happens a few weeks after contracts are exchanged, and that's the moment the keys change hands and the sale is complete.

What does it cost to sell a house in Adelaide?

Beyond agent commission, it's worth budgeting for marketing, conveyancing and any styling or minor repairs. A good agent will set these out clearly up front so there are no surprises, and show you how each marketing dollar is working towards your final result.

It also helps to understand how commission is structured. A slightly higher fee paired with a strong marketing campaign and a skilled negotiator can easily net you more than a bargain-rate service that leaves money on the table. If you'd like a closer look at how value is assessed, our guide to accurate property value appraisal is a great place to start.

How long does it take to sell a house in Adelaide?

Timeframes vary with the season, your suburb and how the property is priced, but a well-presented, sensibly priced Adelaide home often finds a buyer within the first few weeks of the campaign. Auctions run to a fixed date, usually around four weeks from launch, while private-treaty sales can move faster or slower depending on buyer demand.

The best way to shorten the process is to get the fundamentals right from day one: an accurate price, standout presentation and marketing that reaches the right buyers.
